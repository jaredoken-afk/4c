Sustainability

Carbon is one of our four pillars, not an afterthought.

Manufacturing precision doesn't just save time and cost. It's also one of the most effective ways to cut the embodied and operational carbon of a construction project.

Embodied Carbon

Less waste, by design.

Conventional stick-framing generates significant offcut waste and material overuse on every jobsite. Because 4C panels are cut and assembled in a controlled factory environment, material use is precisely engineered to each project's plans, reducing scrap and the embodied carbon that comes with it. This is true of both our 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ systems, and you can see the manufacturing process in the Borrego Springs assembly time-lapse on our Project Portfolio.

Operational Carbon

Thermal performance that lowers long-term energy use.

4C ECO™ is engineered for the highest thermal efficiency in our product line, helping reduce a building's ongoing heating and cooling energy demand long after construction wraps. Combined with faster dry-in, better-sealed building envelopes reduce energy loss from day one.

Resilience As Sustainability

The most sustainable building is the one you don't have to rebuild.

4C SAFE®'s fully non-combustible, 1-HR and 2-HR fire-rated construction is a sustainability strategy in its own right: every home that survives a wildfire is carbon that never needs to be spent on a rebuild. That's part of why our panel systems are central to fire recovery efforts in Altadena and the Pacific Palisades. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does prefabrication reduce carbon compared to site-built construction?

Factory manufacturing allows precise material cutting and reuse of offcuts, dramatically reducing the construction waste that's typical of site-built framing, while shorter onsite schedules reduce equipment idle time and site energy use.

Is 4C ECO™ more sustainable than 4C SAFE®?

4C ECO™ is optimized for thermal efficiency, which can reduce a building's long-term operational energy use. 4C SAFE®'s non-combustible steel frame supports long-term resilience, reducing the carbon and cost impact of rebuilding after fire damage. The better fit depends on your project's risk profile: see our Systems overview to compare.

Does building with 4C panels help with LEED, CALGreen, or other green building certifications?

4C panels reduce onsite construction waste and improve thermal performance, both of which can support credits under green building programs like CALGreen or LEED. Talk to your architect or certifying body about how factory-manufactured panel systems apply to your specific certification pathway.

Does faster onsite construction actually reduce a project's carbon footprint?

Yes. Cutting onsite construction time by up to 30% means less equipment idling, fewer job-site deliveries, and shorter exposure to weather-related material waste, all of which reduce a project's overall carbon footprint compared to conventional stick-framing.

How does prefabricated panel construction reduce embodied carbon compared to rebuilding after a fire?

Every material, appliance, and finish lost in a fire carries embodied carbon that has to be replaced. Building with a fully non-combustible, 1-HR and 2-HR fire-rated, WUI compliant system like 4C SAFE® reduces the odds of a total loss, which means the embodied carbon already invested in the home is less likely to need replacing.

Can developers or architects request embodied carbon data for a 4C project?

Contact our team to discuss project-specific documentation. As part of our end-to-end process, we work directly with owners, architects, and builders to support the data and specifications their projects require.

How does running one coordinated process reduce waste compared to disconnected trades?

Working directly with owners, architects, and builders lets our component systems be configured into a project's existing design and engineering from the start, rather than retrofitted, which keeps material waste and rework to a minimum. Learn more on our Who We Serve page.
