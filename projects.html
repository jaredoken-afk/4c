FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 4C projects have been completed in California?

We've delivered projects across Borrego Springs, Altadena, Escondido, and Los Angeles, ranging from single-family homes to ADUs, with more in progress as part of our fire rebuild program.

Can I see a 4C project in person before specifying it on my own?

Contact our team to discuss a site visit or reference project relevant to your building type and location.

What is the Behind the Build video showing?

The Behind the Build video is a real time-lapse of the Borrego Springs Residence, our first-ever 4C SAFE® installation. It shows panels manufactured in Hayward being delivered to a remote desert site and assembled without heavy equipment, from panel layout through standing frame.

Are all the locations on this page finished projects?

The projects shown here, in Altadena, Los Angeles, Escondido, and Borrego Springs, are completed builds. Our fire rebuild program in Pacific Palisades is in progress and doesn't yet have completed projects to show, and our San Francisco Bay Area and Sacramento markets are newer with no completed projects yet either.

How long did the Borrego Springs installation take from panels arriving to a standing frame?

The Behind the Build video documents the full sequence, from pre-labeled panels laid out on site to a completed steel frame with roof framing installed, without any heavy equipment. Both 4C systems are engineered to cut onsite construction time by up to 30% versus stick-framing.

Do you have completed projects using 4C ECO™ as well as 4C SAFE®?

Yes. Our Escondido Residence was built on the 4C ECO™ system, while our Altadena, Los Angeles, and Borrego Springs projects used the 4C SAFE® system. Both systems share the same manufacturing platform and installation speed.

Can I request a reference project near my own site before I commit to a system?

Yes. Our team can point you to the completed or in-progress project closest to your building type, location, and fire-risk profile, whether that's a fire rebuild, ADU, single-family home, or multifamily infill.
