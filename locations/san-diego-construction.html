Local Market

One county, two very different climates.

San Diego County isn't one construction market. It's several. Coastal and inland-suburban communities like Escondido call for efficient, well-insulated homes that balance upfront cost with long-term energy performance. Further east, the desert interior around Borrego Springs presents extreme temperature swings and a remote logistics environment where a system that arrives pre-engineered and requires no heavy equipment to assemble is a genuine advantage. Our systems overview compares both options side by side if you're weighing which one fits your site.

We've already built in both settings. Our Escondido project used 4C ECO™ for its thermal efficiency and budget control, while our Borrego Springs project was a single-family fire rebuild and the very first 4C SAFE® installation anywhere, proof that both systems hold up across the county's full range of conditions. That kind of factory discipline is central to our mission as a manufacturer, not just a materials supplier.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 4C Construction System offer construction in San Diego?

Yes. 4C delivers prefabricated panel construction across San Diego County, from coastal Escondido to desert-climate Borrego Springs, using our ICC-ES listed 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ systems.

Does 4C Construction System build in San Diego County?

Yes. We've completed projects across San Diego County, including homes in Borrego Springs and Escondido, and we deliver panelized systems throughout the county from our Hayward, CA manufacturing facility.

Where was 4C SAFE® first installed?

Our first-ever 4C SAFE® installation was a 1,090 sq ft, 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom single-family fire rebuild in Borrego Springs, in San Diego County's desert interior.

What was built with 4C ECO™ in Escondido?

We delivered a 1,894 sq ft residence in Escondido with 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, an office, and a carport, built on 4C ECO™ for balanced cost and thermal performance.

Can 4C systems handle San Diego County's range of climates?

Yes. San Diego County spans coastal, inland-suburban, and desert conditions, and both 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ are engineered to perform across that range, as demonstrated by our completed projects in Escondido and Borrego Springs.

Do I need to change my architectural plans to use 4C panels in San Diego?

No. 4C systems integrate with conventional design, financing, and permitting workflows, so San Diego County architects, developers, and builders can specify panelized construction without redesigning a project from scratch.

How do 4C panels get delivered to a remote site like Borrego Springs?

Panels ship from our Hayward, CA manufacturing facility pre-labeled and sequenced for assembly, then travel by truck to the job site. Because no heavy equipment is required to assemble them, remote desert sites like Borrego Springs don't need a crane or specialty rigging on standby.

Can a GC or developer buy 4C panels directly, or only through an architect?

Either can specify our systems. We sell exclusively through partners, which includes architects, GCs, developers, and material vendors, but not directly to homeowners. Whoever is leading your San Diego County project can bring us in.

Are 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ both approved for use in San Diego County jurisdictions?

Both systems carry ICC-ES listings, 4C SAFE® under ESL-1695 and 4C ECO™ under ESR-3373, which local building departments across San Diego County can reference during plan review and permitting.
