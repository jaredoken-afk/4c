Why Speed Matters Right Now

LA can't rebuild at the pace the fires demand.

The January 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires destroyed more than 16,000 structures and caused an estimated $275 billion in damage, one of the costliest disasters in U.S. history. A year later, fewer than 30 of the roughly 1,420 rebuild projects underway had actually been completed, and more than 70% of displaced residents were still displaced, slowed by the same labor shortages, material cost spikes, and permitting bottlenecks facing every stick-framed rebuild competing for the same crews and materials at once.

4C exists to break that bottleneck. Components are manufactured off-site at our Hayward facility while permitting moves in parallel, so your project isn't waiting in line for the same constrained labor pool as every other rebuild in the county. That cuts onsite construction time by up to 30% versus conventional stick-framing, without asking architects or homeowners to change how a project is designed, financed, or permitted.

Local Market

A market with two very different challenges.

Los Angeles is one of the largest and most varied construction markets in the country, and it puts two very different demands on a building system. In the hillside and canyon communities that ring the basin, much of the housing stock sits inside high fire-hazard severity zones, where non-combustible construction and fire-resistive assemblies aren't optional extras: they're the baseline. At the same time, LA is home to one of the most active accessory dwelling unit and infill markets in California, where developers and homeowners need to add square footage quickly, on tight lots, without blowing a budget. Reducing embodied carbon and material waste is part of that equation too, which is why sustainability is built into how both systems are manufactured.

4C Construction System manufactures a panel system for each side of that equation. Both arrive pre-labeled and pre-engineered, require no heavy equipment to assemble, and integrate with the design, financing, and permitting process an LA architect or developer has already built. No redesign is required. That efficiency ties directly back to our four pillars of cost, carbon, complexity, and compliance.

4C SAFE® for wildfire-interface lots

Steel-framed and fully non-combustible with a 1-HR and 2-HR fire rating (ICC-ES ESL-1695, patent pending), built for the hillside and canyon neighborhoods where fire hazard severity is highest.

4C ECO™ for infill and ADUs

Wood-framed Type V construction (ICC-ES ESR-3373) optimized for thermal efficiency and budget control, a strong fit for LA's multifamily and ADU pipeline.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 4C Construction System offer construction in Los Angeles?

Yes. 4C delivers prefabricated panel construction across Los Angeles County, from hillside and fire rebuilds to multifamily infill and ADUs, using our ICC-ES listed 4C SAFE® steel and 4C ECO™ wood systems.

Does 4C Construction System deliver to Los Angeles County?

Yes. We manufacture at our Hayward, CA facility and deliver panelized systems to job sites throughout Los Angeles County, from coastal neighborhoods to inland and hillside communities.

Which 4C system is right for a hillside or wildfire-interface property in LA?

For hillside and canyon properties in higher fire-hazard areas, we typically recommend 4C SAFE®, our steel-framed, fully non-combustible, 1-HR and 2-HR fire-rated system. It's applicable to all construction types and built for wildfire-prone conditions.

Can 4C panels be used for ADUs in Los Angeles?

Yes. 4C ECO™, our wood-framed Type V system, is well suited to ADUs and multifamily infill, offering strong thermal efficiency and better budget control for LA's active accessory dwelling unit market.

Has 4C Construction System completed projects in Los Angeles?

Yes. We've delivered a 2,535 sq ft single-family residence in Los Angeles with 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, an office, and a 2-car garage, built on the 4C panel platform.

Do 4C panels require redesigning plans that were drawn for stick-framing?

No. 4C systems are engineered to integrate with conventional design, financing, and permitting workflows, so LA architects and developers can specify panelized construction without reworking an existing project.

Can homeowners in Los Angeles buy directly from 4C Construction System?

No. We sell exclusively through partners, architects, GCs, developers, and material vendors, rather than direct to homeowners. If you're planning a project, your architect or contractor can bring 4C panels into the design and specify them on your behalf.

Do 4C panels work for a rebuild after the Palisades or Eaton fires?

Yes. We support fire-rebuild projects with dedicated resources for Pacific Palisades and Altadena, both within Los Angeles County, and 4C SAFE®'s non-combustible construction is a natural fit for rebuilding in a high fire-hazard severity zone.

How much faster is panelized construction than a typical LA stick-frame build?

Both 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ cut onsite construction time by up to 30% compared to conventional stick-framing, largely because panels arrive pre-labeled and pre-engineered instead of being framed board by board on site.
