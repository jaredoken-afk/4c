FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who does 4C Construction System work with?

We serve three kinds of customers: owners and property developers, architects and designers, and builders and general contractors. We also work with a network of material and product vendors. If you're a homeowner, contact us and we'll refer you to a partner in your area.

Who can become a 4C vendor or channel partner?

We work with licensed architecture and design firms, licensed general contractors and builders, residential and multifamily developers, and material or product vendors whose offerings complement our component systems.

Is there a cost to apply?

There's no fee to apply. We review each application to confirm licensing and project fit before onboarding.

Do partners get training on the 4C systems?

Yes. Partners receive technical training on both 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ systems, along with ongoing support from our engineering team throughout the process.

Is there a minimum project volume to work with 4C?

No. We evaluate applications on licensing and project fit rather than a minimum volume commitment. Many partners start with a single project, such as a fire rebuild or ADU, before scaling up to multiple projects.

Can a general contractor and an architect both work with 4C on the same project?

Yes. It's common for an architecture firm to run a project through configuration and permit documentation while a general contractor partner handles delivery and installation. Our team coordinates with both throughout the process.

How do partners get access to project pricing and lead times?

Once onboarded, partners work directly with our team to get project-specific pricing and delivery schedules based on component type, project size, and site location. Contact us to start that conversation.

Do partners need special equipment or certifications to install 4C components?

No heavy equipment is required to install either system. Partner crews complete our technical training program, which covers component handling, sequencing, and installation for both 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™.
