Step by Step

Nine steps, one coordinated process.

This is the same process our owners, architects, and builders follow on every project, from first fire rebuilds in Altadena to new builds across the state. Steps 1–8 are the same whether you choose Components Only or Full-Service; step 9 is where the two paths differ.

Step 01

Building Design

Every project starts with a design, not with our system. Bring us architectural drawings at any stage, from an early concept sketch to a fully permitted set, and we work from what you already have. No design style, unit mix, or construction type is off-limits, and there's no requirement to redesign around a proprietary system.

This is one of only two steps in the process that's entirely custom to your project. See how the rest runs on a proven, repeatable engine in the process breakdown below.

Component Configuration
Step 02

Configuration

Our configurator takes your building design and converts it into defined components and specifications: wall assemblies, framing members, insulation, sheathing, and the exact quantities and dimensions your project needs.

This is where a design becomes a buildable, manufacturable scope, without asking you to specify manufacturing details yourself.

Manufacturing Prep
Step 03

Manufacturing Preparation

Once your project is configured, we translate that scope into the actual parts list and manufacturing instructions our Hayward facility runs on: cut lists, assembly sequences, and the labeling that determines exactly how each component gets built and marked for installation order.

This is the step that turns a specification into a production plan.

Digital Building Model
Step 04

Digital Model

We produce a single, coordinated digital model that ties your design intent to the structure and every component in it. That model becomes the source of truth for the rest of the process, permitting, certification, and manufacturing, so nothing drifts between what was designed and what gets built.

Permit Set
Step 05

Permit Documentation

We generate the permit and construction drawing set your jurisdiction requires, built directly from the same coordinated digital model rather than a separate, disconnected drawing effort.

That keeps what gets submitted for approval in sync with what actually gets manufactured and installed.

Engineering Certification
Step 06

Certification and Support

Every project is backed by engineering certification and technical documentation for both our 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ component systems, both ICC-ES listed.

Our team stays involved past this point too, providing ongoing project support through manufacturing, delivery, and installation.

Step 07

Manufacturing

Components are manufactured at our Hayward, CA facility to your project's exact specifications, under either our 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™ system depending on your configuration.

Every component is labeled for its installation sequence before it ever leaves the facility. See what we manufacture in more detail below.

Step 08

Delivery

Pre-labeled components are delivered directly to your job site, sequenced for installation. There's no staging yard and no guesswork about what arrives when: every delivery is coordinated against your installation schedule.

Step 09

Installation

This is the step where Components Only and Full-Service diverge. If you chose Full-Service, our team manages installation directly. If you chose Components Only, your crew installs from here, following the same labeled sequence, without heavy equipment.

Either way, onsite construction time is cut by up to 30% versus conventional stick-framing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the steps in 4C Construction System's process?

Building design, configuration, manufacturing preparation, digital model, permit documentation, certification and support, manufacturing, delivery, and installation. It's one coordinated process from your initial design through to manufacturing, delivery, and installation.

What does the configurator actually do?

The configurator converts your architectural or building design into defined building components and specifications, which then drive manufacturing preparation, the digital model, and permit documentation.

Do I need to change my architectural design to use this process?

No. Start with any building design. Our configurator adapts it into manufacturable components and specifications, so you don't need to redesign a project from scratch. Contact us to review your plans.

What components does 4C Construction System actually manufacture?

Depending on your project's configuration, components are manufactured under our 4C SAFE® (steel-framed, fully non-combustible, 1-HR fire-rated) or 4C ECO™ (wood-framed, Type V) systems, both ICC-ES listed.

Can I just buy the components and install them myself?

Yes. That's our Components Only path: we configure, manufacture, and deliver certified, labeled components, and your own crew or GC handles installation. If you'd rather we manage installation too, that's our Full-Service path. Both follow the same process through delivery.

Do I need heavy equipment to install the components?

No. Components arrive pre-labeled and sized for crews to install by hand, following a sequence similar to a well-engineered kit, whether your own crew installs them or our team manages that step directly.

Where does manufacturing happen?

Components are manufactured at our facility in Hayward, CA, to exact project specifications, then labeled for installation sequence before delivery to your job site anywhere in California.

Can I see a real project built through this process?

Yes. Our project portfolio includes completed builds, including the Borrego Springs residence, the first-ever 4C SAFE® installation, which has a full assembly time-lapse video.
