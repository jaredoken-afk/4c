FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote from 4C Construction System?

Fill out the request form on this page with your project details, or call us at 650-200-3182 or email info@4ccs.com. We'll follow up within one business day.

How quickly will I hear back after submitting a quote request?

We aim to follow up within one business day of receiving your project details, whether submitted through our online form, by phone, or by email.

I'm a homeowner. Can I contact 4C Construction System directly for my project?

4C Construction System works directly with owners and property developers, architects and designers, and builders and general contractors. If you're a homeowner, reach out and we'll help connect you with a partner in your area.

What information should I include when requesting a quote?

Include your project location, project type, and timeline, along with whether you're an owner or developer, an architect or designer, or a builder or general contractor. If you already know whether you need 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™, let us know; otherwise we can help you decide by reviewing our process overview.

Where is 4C Construction System's office located?

Our headquarters and manufacturing facility are at 2447 Industrial Parkway, Hayward, CA 94545, in the San Francisco Bay Area. Learn more about our company.

Can I request a quote if my project is a fire rebuild?

Yes. Note that your project is a fire rebuild in the project details field. We run a dedicated rebuild program for homeowners and builders in fire-affected communities including Altadena and Pacific Palisades.

Does 4C Construction System serve my area of California?

We manufacture in Hayward and deliver statewide, with active project experience in the San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, San Diego, Sacramento, Altadena, and Pacific Palisades. See our full list of California locations to confirm coverage for your area.
