FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 4C Construction System deliver outside the cities listed here?

Yes. We deliver throughout California from our Hayward manufacturing facility. If your city isn't listed above, contact us and we'll confirm delivery logistics for your specific site.

Do I need a local contractor to build with 4C panels, or can any California GC use your system?

Any licensed California general contractor can build with 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™ once they're part of our partner network. If you already work with a GC, we can bring them on as a partner, or refer you to one of our existing partners in your area.

Are delivery times different for coastal, hillside, or inland sites?

Site access affects scheduling more than distance does. Because 4C panels arrive pre-labeled, pre-engineered, and require no heavy equipment to assemble, they're well suited to constrained hillside or canyon lots like those in Altadena and Pacific Palisades, though we confirm logistics for every site individually.

Which 4C system is used in each location?

Project needs, not location, determine the system. Our fire-rebuild programs in Altadena and Pacific Palisades use 4C SAFE® for its 1-HR and 2-HR fire rating and WUI compliance, while other California markets may use either 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™ depending on the project. See our systems overview for a full comparison.

Is 4C Construction System licensed to operate in all of these locations?

Yes. We hold CSLB license #1145002 and CAB certification #N41299, which apply statewide across California, including every market and fire-rebuild location listed on this page.

Can I see completed projects near my location before choosing a system?

Yes. Our project portfolio includes completed builds in Altadena, Los Angeles, Escondido, and Borrego Springs, including a "Behind the Build" timelapse video of our first-ever 4C SAFE® install.

How do I get started on a project in my city?

Since 4C sells exclusively through partners, the fastest path is to work with an architect, general contractor, or developer already in our network, or to become a partner yourself. Either way, contact us and we'll point you in the right direction.
