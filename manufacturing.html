Overview

Where specification becomes a physical component.

Manufacturing is the seventh of our nine process steps, and the first step where your project becomes something physical. Everything upstream, configuration, manufacturing preparation, the digital model, permit documentation, and certification, exists to make sure manufacturing runs against a locked, accurate specification rather than a moving target.

Components are cut, assembled, and finished to that specification under either our 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™ system, then labeled for their exact position in the installation sequence before they ever leave the facility.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does 4C Construction System manufacture components?

At our facility at 2447 Industrial Parkway, Hayward, CA 94545, in the San Francisco Bay Area, then delivered to job sites statewide.

What happens before manufacturing starts?

Manufacturing is step 7 of our nine-step process. It follows configuration, manufacturing preparation, the digital model, permit documentation, and certification, so by the time a component is manufactured its specification, permit set, and engineering certification are already locked in.

How does 4C keep manufacturing consistent across projects?

Every component is built to the same documented, repeatable manufacturing standard, under either our 4C SAFE® or 4C ECO™ system, both ICC-ES listed, so what's specified in configuration is what actually gets manufactured.

What does labeling for installation sequence mean?

Each component is labeled during manufacturing with its position and order in the installation sequence, so crews on site can install without guessing what comes next or needing a staging yard to sort components.

What's the difference between 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™ manufacturing?

Both are manufactured to the same documented standard at our Hayward facility. 4C SAFE® is steel-framed and fully non-combustible, ICC-ES listed under ESL-1695. 4C ECO™ is wood-framed and optimized for Type V construction, ICC-ES listed under ESR-3373.

What happens after manufacturing?

Components move to delivery, coordinated directly to your job site. From there it depends on which path you chose: Components Only, where your own crew or GC handles installation, or Full-Service, where our team manages installation directly. See the two ways to work with us for details.

Can I just buy manufactured components and install them myself?

Yes. That's our Components Only path: we configure, manufacture, and deliver certified, labeled components, and your own crew or GC handles installation from there. Choose Full-Service instead if you want our team to manage installation too.
