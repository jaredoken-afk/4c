About Us

Turning building designs into certified, manufactured components.

We're a Hayward, California-based company providing a complete, end-to-end service that turns building designs into manufactured, certified, and installation-ready building components, built to make construction faster, cheaper, and more consistent for the owners, architects, and builders who work with us.

Our Mission

One coordinated process, from design through installation.

Traditional construction relies on dozens of trades, materials, and disconnected handoffs all coming together perfectly, on schedule. We replace that complexity with one coordinated process: configuration, manufacturing preparation, a coordinated digital model, permit documentation, certification, manufacturing, delivery, and installation support, without asking architects, owners, or developers to change how they design, finance, or permit a project. Explore how our process works to see how that plays out in practice.

“A Better Way to Build Without Changing the Building: 4C replaces multiple materials and on-site trades with a standardized, factory-built system that delivers faster dry-in, higher performance, and greater certainty. The building itself, from design and permitting to financing and inspection, remains entirely conventional.”

The 4C Standard

Four pillars, one name.

Every decision we make across our end-to-end process is measured against these four principles, including how we serve owners, architects, and builders who work with us.

01

Cost

Reducing upfront, operational, and long-term costs by shifting labor from the jobsite to the factory floor.

02

Carbon

Minimizing embodied and operational carbon through efficient material use and dramatically less onsite construction waste.

03

Complexity

Consolidating a dozen site-built trades into a single, engineered component system that arrives ready to install.

04

Compliance

Delivering documented, code-compliant, ICC-ES listed performance so inspections go the way you planned.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is 4C Construction System based?

Our headquarters and manufacturing facility are located at 2447 Industrial Parkway, Hayward, CA 94545, in the San Francisco Bay Area. We deliver projects throughout California.

What does the name "4C" stand for?

4C represents four pillars behind every project we deliver: Cost, Carbon, Complexity, and Compliance.

Is 4C Construction System licensed and certified?

Yes. We hold CSLB License #1145002 and CAB #N41299, and both of our component systems carry ICC-ES listings (ESL-1695 for 4C SAFE®, ESR-3373 for 4C ECO™).

Does 4C Construction System manufacture the components itself, or outsource production?

We manufacture components ourselves at our Hayward, CA facility, as one step in our end-to-end process. Keeping production in-house is how we hold every component to the same documented, repeatable standard across both 4C SAFE® and 4C ECO™.

How long has 4C Construction System been building projects in California?

We've delivered completed projects across California including single-family homes in Los Angeles and Escondido, fire rebuilds and an ADU in Altadena, and our first-ever 4C SAFE® installation in Borrego Springs. See the full record on our project portfolio.

Does 4C Construction System sell directly to homeowners?

No. We sell exclusively through our partner network of architects, general contractors, developers, and material vendors. Homeowners interested in a 4C project should work with one of our partners.

How does 4C Construction System address embodied carbon in its components?

Carbon is one of our four founding pillars. We minimize embodied and operational carbon through efficient material use and factory manufacturing, which produces significantly less onsite construction waste than conventional stick-framing. Read more on our sustainability page.
