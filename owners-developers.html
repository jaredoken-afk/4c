The Process

From concept to occupancy.

This is how a typical project moves once you bring it to us.

1

Concept & Feasibility

Share your building design or project concept. We assess configuration options and give you an early read on cost and schedule.

2

Configuration & Budgeting

Our configurator defines components and specifications, locking in a firm scope before you commit further capital.

3

Permitting & Certification

We generate permit documentation and engineering certification alongside your approvals process, instead of after it.

4

Manufacturing

Components are manufactured to spec at our Hayward facility while your approvals and site work proceed in parallel.

5

Delivery & Occupancy

Coordinated delivery and installation support keep the project moving toward a predictable occupancy date.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How early can I get firm cost and schedule numbers?

As soon as your project is configured, early in the process and before permitting begins, so you're committing capital against a defined scope rather than an estimate.

Do I need an architect before I can talk to 4C?

No. You can start with any concept or existing design. We can work with an architect of your choice or, if you don't have one, refer you to one of our architecture partners.

How does this reduce risk on my project compared to conventional construction?

Configuration, permitting, certification, and manufacturing run as one coordinated process instead of disconnected handoffs, so cost and schedule are locked in earlier and there are fewer surprises once construction starts.

Can this work alongside my existing general contractor or development team?

Yes. It's common for a developer to bring their own general contractor for site work and delivery scheduling while our team handles configuration, certification, and manufacturing.

Does this process work for fire rebuild or ADU projects?

Yes. We run a dedicated rebuild program for homeowners and developers in fire-affected communities including Altadena and the Pacific Palisades, alongside standard new-build and ADU projects.

Where are the components for my project manufactured?

At our facility in Hayward, CA, to your project's exact specifications, then delivered to your site anywhere in California.
